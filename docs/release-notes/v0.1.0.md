The first release without known crashes or obvious graphical glitches. plethron is a PC port of **Pocket Monsters
Stadium** (N64, Japan, 1998), built on the team-hippodrome decompilation and libultraship. It is preservation work:
the game plays as it did on the N64, and everything new is an option, off unless you turn it on.

**No game data is included.** You need your own dump of the original Japanese ROM (`.z64`); Game Boy carts for the
GB Player and Transfer Pak are your own dumps too.

## New since v0.0.1

- **Optional enhancements** (launcher > Graphics > Enhancements, all off by default), from
  [hippodrome-utils](https://github.com/team-hippodrome/hippodrome-utils):
  - Summary bars: a card per Pokémon beside the picture with its battle model, HP, status, the moves it has
    revealed and an estimate of its Speed from the turn order. They need room beside the picture (4:3 in a wide
    window, or 16:9 on an ultrawide screen).
  - Trainer memory: press C on the cup select (L1-30) or the L50 menu to see what each trainer's Pokémon used
    against you before. Kept in `<save>.memory` beside the N64 save.
- **Staff roll** names are translated (romaji, Hangul in Korean, kanji in Japanese with kanji).
- **Announcer subtitles** checked against the announcer's audio: the Self-Destruct/Explosion line, the low-HP line
  and a few others now say what he says.
- **Widescreen:** the menus' tiled Poké Ball background now reaches the 16:9 sides (main menu, order screen,
  Pokédex), and the badge cap no longer sits behind the semifinal VS panels.
- **Battle graphics:** Moltres's crest flame is drawn on its battle thumbnail again, and Hyper Beam's strands
  match the N64.
- **Pokédex:** every species played the next one's cry (Charizard sounded like Squirtle); fixed, and the cry row
  and the clear-save option are translated.

## Since v0.0.1 was re-published

v0.0.1 first shipped without its fonts and translations (blank language select). They are now built into the
executable, so the download is still a single file per platform; a loose `translations/` folder next to it wins
over the built-in copy.

## Builds

- Windows x64 and arm64 (zip: `plethron.exe`)
- Linux x86_64 AppImage (`chmod +x` after downloading)

There is no macOS build yet: the port's lifted game data is linked through GNU ld aliases that Apple's linker does
not read.

## Known differences

- Sleep Powder and Haze look different from the N64 (the accurate colour combiner looked worse, so it was kept).
- When a Pokémon faints, no cry plays. This is how Zero does it on the N64 too.
- Articuno's Fly camera doesn't follow it up. Also original behaviour.
