First public test build of plethron, a PC port of **Pocket Monsters Stadium** (N64, Japan, 1998), built on the
team-hippodrome decompilation and libultraship. This is preservation work: the game plays as it did on the N64,
and anything new (widescreen, translations, fonts, subtitles) is an option.

**No game data is included.** You need your own dump of the original Japanese ROM (`.z64`); the port checks it and
refuses other revisions, patched or translated ROMs. Game Boy carts for the GB Player and Transfer Pak are your own
dumps too.

## What's in it

- Language select before the title screen: にほんご (kana) as on the cartridge; press C-left for AI translations
  into Japanese with kanji, English, Spanish, German, Portuguese, Korean, French and Italian.
  Human corrections in `translations/human.csv` take priority over the AI text.
- Announcer subtitles, translated move names, trainer classes, COM nicknames (localized or transliterated,
  Options > Names) and the GB Player's own screens.
- GB Player with a built-in Game Boy core; Transfer Pak carts from every region (Western saves are converted, full
  Western nicknames kept next to the N64 save).
- Widescreen (16:9) battles, VS scenes and menus, controllers 1 to 4, sound and music volume sliders.
- A launcher to pick the ROM, the save and the Transfer Pak carts, with the cart's Pokémon drawn from your own ROM.
- Saves stay in the N64's 32 KiB SRAM format, so they move between the port and emulators.

## Builds

- Windows x64 and arm64 (zip: `pokestadium.exe`)
- Linux x86_64 AppImage (`chmod +x` after downloading)

There is no macOS build yet: the port's lifted game data is linked through GNU ld aliases that Apple's linker
does not read.

## Known differences and limits

- Sleep Powder and Haze look different from the N64 (the accurate colour combiner looked worse, so it was kept).
- This is v0.0.1: expect bugs. Reports with the language, screen and save are welcome as issues.
